This Week's Projections

Each team's own regression, evaluated against what its opponent has allowed this season — compared against the market line. Edge is how many points the model sits from the line; it is a size of disagreement, not a confidence score. Market win chance comes from the moneyline with the bookmaker's margin removed, so it is the market's estimate, not the model's. Spread confidence rates how well the two teams' regressions fit their own games: Strong means both teams average R² 0.67 or higher and a ± under 5.8, Weak means both are worse than that, and Average is anything in between. Total confidence is Weak when the model's total is 8 or more points from the posted total; those over/under picks have mostly lost this season. Both ratings come from this season's graded games.
